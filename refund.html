TablePull — Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

1. Overview

Because TablePull Pro is a digital license key that unlocks software features immediately upon activation, all Pro purchases are non-refundable once the license key has been activated.

2. When a refund may be granted

A refund may be considered only if: (a) the license key has not been activated within 7 days of purchase, and (b) you report a defect that makes the core extraction feature unusable on a supported Chrome version and we cannot resolve it. Requests must be sent to privacy@tablepull.app within 7 days of purchase.

3. How to request

Email privacy@tablepull.app with your order reference and the email used at checkout. Approved refunds are processed by our Merchant of Record (Dodo Payments) and appear on your statement within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

4. Free tier

The free tier requires no payment and no refund applies.
