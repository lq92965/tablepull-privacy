ComplianceGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29 · Effective immediately

Our principle

ComplianceGuard is a Shopify app that scans product content for material that may need an AI disclosure under EU AI Act Article 50, and writes a disclosure to a product metafield. It does not access customer or order data at all. The app requests only read_products, write_products and, optionally, read_markets.

What we collect

To operate the app we store, per installed store:

  • Your shop domain (for example your-store.myshopify.com).
  • An offline access token issued by Shopify, plus its refresh token, used to call the Shopify Admin API on your behalf. Tokens expire and are refreshed automatically.
  • A short run log: timestamp, operation type (scan or label), and the number of products processed.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, order data, payment data, browsing history, or analytics. There is no account and no password.

Your product data

Product titles, handles, descriptions, images and variant weights are read from the Shopify Admin API at the moment you press Scan, and are returned to your browser. They are not written to our database. The only write operation the app performs is setting the metafield custom.ai_disclosure on the products you explicitly select and confirm.

Where data is stored

The token and run log are stored on a single server operated by us (DigitalOcean, United States), in a file readable only by the application process. Transmission between your browser, Shopify and our server uses HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while the app is installed. When the app is uninstalled, Shopify sends us a shop/redact webhook 48 hours later and we delete the token and all run logs for that store. You can also request deletion at any time by emailing us; we action it within 30 days.

GDPR and CCPA

We respond to Shopify's mandatory compliance webhooks: customers/data_request, customers/redact and shop/redact. Because the app never receives customer data, the first two always resolve to "no data held".

Permissions and why

  • read_products — to scan product descriptions, images and variants for disclosure gaps.
  • write_products — to write the custom.ai_disclosure metafield when you apply labels.
  • read_markets (optional) — only to determine whether your store sells into the EU. If declined, the app reports EU exposure as unknown and still works.

Third parties

We share no data with third parties. The only external service involved is Shopify itself (Admin API, and Billing for subscription charges). Payments are processed by Shopify and appear on your Shopify invoice; we never see or store card details.
