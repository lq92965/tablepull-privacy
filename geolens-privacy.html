GeoLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23 · Effective immediately

Our principle

GeoLens is a browser extension that scores any product or store page for SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) visibility and, with your own AI key, suggests optimized titles, meta descriptions, FAQ schema and a citeable blurb. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers, because we do not operate any servers that receive your data. Your page content, settings, and results stay 100% locally inside your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect names, emails, the pages you scan, generated content, browsing history, or usage analytics. There is no account and no backend database that receives your data.

The only purchase-related data involved is your license key, which is sent to the license-verification endpoint (Dodo Payments) solely to validate that your Pro purchase is active. We do not store your license key on any server.

The pages you scan

When you click "Scan this page", GeoLens reads only the page you are currently viewing, using the activeTab permission — it does not read other tabs or websites in the background. The extracted page text is stored only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device and is never uploaded to us.

Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) AI features

The "AI optimize" feature uses your own LLM API key. That key is stored only in your browser's sync storage (chrome.storage.sync) and is never sent to us. When you use it, the page content you scanned is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you configured, using your key. We are not an intermediary and never see the key or the content. The local SEO + GEO audit runs entirely offline and sends nothing anywhere.

Permissions and why

  • activeTab — to read the product/store page you are viewing, only when you click "Scan this page".
  • scripting — to run the local extraction script on that page.
  • storage — to remember your settings and BYOK key on your device.
  • Host permission: https://lq92965.github.io/* — read the license key from our own order-complete page after a successful checkout, so Pro unlocks automatically. No page content is read and no other site is accessed.

GeoLens does not request broad host permissions, does not run remotely hosted code, and does not access chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store.

Third parties

We share no data with third parties. The only network requests your browser may make are: (1) at your explicit choice, to the AI provider you configured when you use the BYOK "AI optimize" feature; and (2) to the license-verification endpoint when you activate or validate a Pro license key.

Payments & licensing

GeoLens is offered as a free tier (unlimited local SEO + GEO audits) plus an optional Pro upgrade ($19 one-time) that unlocks the AI optimize feature and .docx export. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, a third-party Merchant of Record — we never see, store, or handle your card or payment details. The checkout is conducted entirely on Dodo's pages.

To unlock Pro, you receive a license key from Dodo and enter it in GeoLens Settings. The key is sent only to Dodo's public license endpoints to confirm it is valid and active. GeoLens stores only the activation status locally on your device (chrome.storage.sync).
