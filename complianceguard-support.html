What the app does

  • Scans your product catalog for content that may need an AI disclosure under EU AI Act Article 50.
  • Reports description length and image count per product, plus whether your store sells into the EU.
  • Writes a disclosure to the product metafield custom.ai_disclosure in bulk (Pro plan).

Where is the label stored?

Shopify admin → Products → pick a product → scroll to Metafields → namespace custom, key ai_disclosure. Your theme reads this value to render the visible label on the product page.

Charges are handled by Shopify and appear on your regular Shopify invoice. To cancel: Shopify admin → Settings → Apps and sales channels → ComplianceGuard → Manage → cancel the subscription. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

Common questions

  • Does the app detect AI-generated text? No tool can do that reliably. The app flags content that would require a disclosure if it was AI-generated, and you decide what to label.
  • Does it modify my products? Not unless you press “Apply labels”. Scanning is read-only and never changes titles, prices, inventory, or orders.
  • Is this legal advice? No. Compliance with Article 50 remains your responsibility.
  • EU exposure shows “unknown”. The optional read_markets scope was not granted. Reinstall and accept it, or confirm your EU exposure manually.
