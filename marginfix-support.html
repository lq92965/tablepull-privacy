What the app does

  • Reads the weight stored on every product variant and normalises g, kg, oz and lb into grams.
  • Flags variants heavier than 2 kg and ranks them, with the exact weight that triggered the flag.
  • Suggests concrete fixes: splitting heavy items, cutting packaging weight, free-shipping thresholds.

Why is my weight showing 0?

Shopify only returns weight if it was entered on the variant. Open Shopify admin → Products → pick a product → Variants → Shipping → set the weight and unit, then run the audit again.

Dimensional weight

Shopify variants do not store dimensions by default, so the app reports actual weight only and tells you where to add L×W×H if you want volumetric calculations.

Common questions

  • Does it change my data? No. The app is read-only; it never edits weights, prices, inventory, or orders.
  • Does it read customers or orders? No. It only requests read_products.
  • Where does the 2 kg threshold come from? It is the point where most carriers step into a higher tier. Treat it as a prioritisation signal, not a hard limit.
