PriceLens: Compare Prices & Holiday Deals - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

PriceLens: Compare Prices & Holiday Deals is a browser extension that runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate a server for this extension and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What the extension does

Compare prices across stores, track lows and catch Black Friday & Christmas deals - all in your browser.

Permissions and why we need them

  • activeTab - read the page you are actively viewing, only when you click a button
  • scripting - run a one-off script in that active tab to read its content
  • storage - save your settings, license and local entries on your device
  • Host permission: https://lq92965.github.io/* — read the license key from our own order-complete page after a successful checkout, so Pro unlocks automatically. No page content is read and no other site is accessed.

Data storage

All settings, license status and entries you create are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage). They never leave your device. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

Third parties

AI features (Pro, optional): if you choose to use AI features, you provide your own API key (BYOK). Requests go directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider under their terms. We never see or store your key.

Payments: Pro licenses are sold via Dodo Payments. Payment data is handled by Dodo under their privacy policy; we only receive a license key.

No tracking, no ads

No analytics, no advertising, no data brokers. We do not collect browsing history.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing.
